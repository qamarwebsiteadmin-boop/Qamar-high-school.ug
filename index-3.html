About Qamar High School

A model school for a holistic education

Vision: A model school offering a holistic education. Mission: to train individuals of moral, ethical and academic integrity who can serve and fit in the dynamic world.

Qamar runs a dual curriculum — secular and theology — and welcomes students of all religions. Swahili is compulsory for every student, and the school offers both O'Level and A'Level under UNEB Centre No. U3697.
